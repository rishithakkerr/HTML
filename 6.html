What are grid lines?
Grid lines are the vertical and horizontal lines that divide the grid into columns and rows.
How many columns does the item span?
The item spans two columns, from grid line 1 to grid line 3.
What happens to other items?
Other items automatically moce to the next available cells.
Can two items overlap?
By default, grid items cannot overlap, unless specifically forced to.
Where is column spanning used in websites?
Column spanning is used for headers, banners, or wide sections.
